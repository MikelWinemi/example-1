My Story

Henrimae Bell.

Henrimae Bell transitioned from a distinguished career in intellectual property law at IBM to founding her own fine art studio. Trained by master artists from Florence, Italy, New York, to Florida, her work blends classical technique with contemporary expression, creating visual narratives that reflect both tradition and innovation.
