II.The Artist

From patent law to painter's studio.

Henrimae H. Bell spent two decades practicing intellectual property law at IBM before answering a call she had carried since childhood. Today, she paints full-time from her studio in the American South.

Her training has taken her from ateliers in Florence and Paris to workshops with master portraitists in West Africa — a lineage of technique that shapes every canvas she signs.

VI.Community Impact · HBCU Give-Back

Ten percent of every sale funds the DLBMSF Scholarship at Albany State University — a Historically Black College and University.

Every original, edition, and scarf you take home routes 10% of the sale directly into scholarship funding at Albany State (Albany, Georgia), an HBCU with more than a century of educating Black leaders. Your purchase is a tuition check for the next generation of thinkers, makers, and artists.
