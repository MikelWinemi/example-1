About Henrimae Bell

From IBM to the canvas.

Henrimae Bell had a long-standing career with International Business Machines (IBM) Corporation at Worldwide Corporate Headquarters in Armonk, New York. She retired from IBM as Worldwide Manager of Information and Support, Intellectual Property Law.

During her corporate career, Henrimae developed leadership and creative problem-solving skills, successfully leading an international team across the United States, France, Germany, the United Kingdom, and Asia. Her team designed and implemented a tracking system for IBM's intellectual properties, generating significant royalties.

Since retiring from IBM, Henrimae transitioned from corporate manager to fine artist and founder of Henrimae Bell's Fine Art Studio. Her work is prominently featured in galleries and interior design shops, including her art studio in historic downtown Melbourne, Florida. She participates in exhibitions across Florida, Georgia, South Carolina, and New York.

Henrimae's artwork has graced the covers of Senior Scene and IndigoLife Magazine. She is also an award-winning artist, guest speaker, and the published author of Strokes of Culture: The Artistic Legacy of Henrimae H. Bell.

Education & Personal Life

Roots in Georgia, home in Florida.

Born & Raised

Born in Cordele, Georgia, and raised between Cordele and Fort Myers, Florida. Currently resides in Indialantic, Florida, and Cordele, Georgia.

Education

Attended Albany State University in Albany, Georgia; earned a B.S. from Iona University in New Rochelle, New York; and a certificate in Graduate Business Management from Pace University.
