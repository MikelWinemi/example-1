My Story

From intellectual property law to the studio.

Henrimae Bell

A second calling.

Before the studio, there were three decades in intellectual property law at IBM — the discipline of the archive, the patience of the case file, the ear for a client's story.

The move to the canvas was less a departure than a promotion of a lifelong second self. Training followed under master artists in Florence, Italy, in New York, and in Florida — hands and eyes recalibrated for oil, pigment, and light.

The work now lives in the tension between those two lives: rigor and reverie, tradition and reinvention.
